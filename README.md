AI-Native Omnichannel ERP Built in Venezuela

Carpihogar is an AI-native omnichannel ERP and commercial operating system designed to centralize business operations, inventory management, commerce, marketing, customer service and operational intelligence within a single platform.

Originally built in Venezuela to solve real-world commercial challenges, Carpihogar has evolved into a production-ready ecosystem capable of supporting brands, inventory investors, marketing partners, physical stores and online commerce through an integrated AI-powered infrastructure.

---

Vision

Our vision is to become the operating system for commerce in Latin America.

Carpihogar combines artificial intelligence, omnichannel commerce, inventory management, CRM, logistics, reporting and commercial automation to help businesses operate more efficiently and scale faster.

Unlike traditional ERP or ecommerce platforms, Carpihogar integrates AI directly into operational workflows rather than limiting it to conversational assistants.

Commercial Ecosystem

Carpihogar operates as a multi-participant commercial ecosystem.

Customers

Purchase products through online, physical and messaging channels.

Inventory Investors

Provide inventory and participate in commercial operations.

Marketing Partners

Create commercial storefronts and generate revenue through commissions.

Brand Managers

Manage supplier relationships, inventory supervision and commercial performance.

Administrators

Control and supervise the complete operational ecosystem.

AI Agents

Automate operational tasks, reporting, marketing and customer assistance.

Each participant has defined responsibilities, incentives and commissions, creating a self-sustaining commercial ecosystem.
